OmniTrust-Med

Trust lifecycle management for healthcare and MedTech.

OmniTrust-Med is focused on helping medical devices, digital health platforms, and connected healthcare systems maintain verifiable trust from design and provisioning through deployment, operation, and audit.

View project on GitHub Website source

What the project emphasizes

  • Secure device, software, and identity lifecycles for healthcare environments.
  • Visibility into trust, integrity, and compliance across connected systems.
  • Support for modern cryptography, automation, and security governance.

Mission

Build a practical foundation for trustworthy healthcare technology by connecting security, lifecycle management, and operational assurance in one place.

Core focus areas

  • Medical device and firmware trust
  • Identity, certificates, keys, and secrets
  • Auditability and compliance readiness
  • Secure cloud and AI-enabled workflows
